Barry

To begin...

Barry was one of those shows that I put off way longer than I should have. I felt like it was a role that I couldn't see Bill Hader in, and I couldn't have been more wrong.

Dealing with personal issues...

"Barry" struggles between the gruesome life of an assassin who's lost purpose and the life of a student in an acting class. While literally juggling body being an assassin and an acting student, this is where Barry seems to have found meaning.

In conclusion,

Between arguably some of the best scenes in both comedy and action, Barry has been a great choice to watch so far. No matter who is directing, I was not prepared for the level and quality of cinematography involved. I have to give a shoutout to Bill Hader; I had no clue that he was a director, and he directed a motorcycle chase scene that was truly awesome to watch. Although I'm excited to finish "Barry", I would hate to rush this masterpiece.
